Cancer Care Initiative

Supporting the most marginalised communities with early cancer detection, awareness, and financial support for treatment — because no one should die from a disease that can be treated.

The Challenge

Cancer is treatable. But for millions, the belief that it isn't becomes a death sentence.

Among the most marginalised communities, cancer carries a fatalism that prevents people from seeking help. The word itself — the name of the disease — is enough to make families give up hope. Myths, stigma, and fear combine with a lack of access to create a devastating gap between what medicine can do and what reaches those who need it most.

For these communities, the barrier is not just affordability — it is awareness, accessibility, and the belief that treatment is even possible. GRAMA's Cancer Care Initiative is designed to address all three.

The idea is simple but transformative: identify cancer as early as possible, break the myths that prevent people from seeking care, and financially support patients through treatment — so that a diagnosis does not become a verdict.

What We Aim To Do

Objectives

Early Detection through Health Camps

Conduct health screening camps directly in panchayats to identify suspected cancer cases at the earliest possible stage, when treatment is most effective.

Break Myths and Build Awareness

Challenge the deeply held belief that cancer is untreatable. Educate communities through local partners so that families seek help instead of giving up.

Financial Support for Treatment

Provide direct financial support to patients from marginalised backgrounds so they can begin and sustain cancer treatment without the burden of unaffordable costs.

Our Partner

Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital and Research Centre, Muzaffarpur

We have collaborated with a senior doctor at Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital and Research Centre in Muzaffarpur which is equipped with advanced facilities to diagnose and treat cancer. The hospital serves as the clinical backbone of this initiative.

Our collective effort brings together the hospital's medical expertise, GRAMA's financial and organisational support, and local partners who understand the communities we serve.
