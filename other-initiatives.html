Beyond governance research and data systems, we facilitate collaborative efforts that bring direct support to the most marginalised communities.

Cancer Care Initiative

A collaborative effort bringing early cancer detection, awareness, and financial support for treatment to communities where a diagnosis is still considered a death sentence.

Early DetectionFinancial Support Muzaffarpur, Bihar4 Partner Collaboration

Among the most marginalised communities, cancer carries a fatalism that prevents people from seeking help. This initiative — a collaboration between a senior oncologist, a grassroots organisation, a cancer hospital, and GRAMA as the facilitator — conducts health camps in panchayats, identifies suspected cases, and financially supports patients through treatment.
