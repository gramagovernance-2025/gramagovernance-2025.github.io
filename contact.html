Current Opening

Research Associate, GRAMA — Bihar Local Governance

Based in Patna  ·  Start: Oct 15, 2026  ·  Apply by: Sept 21, 2026

GRAMA — led by M. R. Sharan (University of Maryland), Siddharth George (National University of Singapore), Siddharth Hari (World Bank), Apurva Bamezai (Ashoka University), and Nandini Gupta (University of Oxford) — is hiring a Research Associate to work across the full arc of a research project: from an RCT on peer learning among local leaders in JEEViKA, Bihar, to translating results for policymakers and seeing how those results shape policy. Previous RAs have gone on to PhD programs at Harvard, Oxford, Chicago, Yale, UBC, Stanford GSB, Columbia, UC Davis, UC Irvine, and Imperial, and to pre-docs at Oxford, Princeton, and Harvard.

What You'll Do

  • Design survey questionnaires and refine surveying instruments
  • Plan and manage data collection, ensuring data quality and productivity
  • Recruit, train, and supervise field-based and data operations teams
  • Clean and analyse data, and prepare documents and presentations for dissemination
  • Engage directly with government stakeholders, from block-level functionaries to senior state officials
  • Contribute to academic papers, policy notes, and presentations for scholarly and government audiences

What We're Looking For

  • Master's or a strong Undergraduate degree in Economics, Political Science, Public Policy, Data Science, or a related field
  • Strong data skills — well-versed in Stata and/or R; working knowledge of Python is a bonus
  • Familiarity with modern applied econometric methods
  • Comfort liaising with a wide range of stakeholders, including senior government officials and academics
  • Willingness to travel within Bihar, including extended stints in the field
  • Working proficiency in English and Hindi; valid work authorization in India
